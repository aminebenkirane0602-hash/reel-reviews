# Reel Reviews

**Product Brief · AI Builder Space Proseminar**
Lebanos Mengistu, Amine Benkirane, Jamie Menhall · October 2, 2026

Reel Reviews makes leaving a review as easy as speaking. Customers record a quick voice review after a visit and earn points, while AI turns everyone's feedback into clear, honest insight for the public and practical recommendations for the business.

## How it works

1. Scan QR code
2. Get a text link
3. Speak the review
4. AI categorizes
5. Insights & summary

## Sprint scope (3–4 weeks)

Goal: a working MVP that proves the full loop with one pilot business type, starting with restaurants.

**Must-haves**
- Customer scans a QR code at a partner business and receives a text with a review link
- Voice review recorded on the web and transcribed into text by AI
- Review must be submitted within 24 hours of the visit
- Basic Reel Review Points, with more points for reviewing sooner
- AI sorts each review into categories (e.g., food, service, wait time, cleanliness, price)
- Business dashboard with categorized feedback and AI recommendations
- Public page showing an AI-generated summary report of the business's reviews

**Out of scope**
- Native mobile app (web only)
- Redeeming points at other participating businesses
- Business-defined incentives and discounts
- Payments, review verification, and fraud detection
- More than one or two pilot businesses

## Success metrics

- **20–30** voice reviews from real pilot customers
- **50%+** of customers who start the flow finish within 24 hours
- **1+** specific change named by the business owner after reviewing the dashboard

## Open questions & risks

- Finding a pilot partner
- Privacy and consent for voice recordings and phone numbers
- Filtering offensive and fake reviews (not built this sprint)
- How Reel Review Points are funded
- FTC disclosure for rewarded reviews
